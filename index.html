Current scope: three layers that should not be conflated

The public repository contains several generations of work. The August 2026 release makes their authority and role explicit so that readers, search engines, and AI systems do not treat historical code, frozen mathematics, and publication infrastructure as one undifferentiated theory.

1. ReIG-DQ mathematical core

ReIG-DQ: Reconstructed Design Theory for Dissipative Quantum Systems is the audited finite-dimensional line organized as T-DQ-01 through T-DQ-05 plus the Identity Bridge.

Status: frozen / publication-ready mathematical core.

2. Legacy ReIG2 / twinRIG

The earlier resonance-operator, phase-transition, SRRFT, LLM integration, simulation, and meaning-field materials remain public as historical and experimental research context.

They are not evidence for a frozen ReIG-DQ theorem unless an explicit dependency is documented.

3. Publication Architecture

Freeze manifests, SHA-256 ledgers, verifier provenance, bilingual synchronization, and GitHub/Zenodo coordination form a separate reproducibility layer around the research outputs.

Publication Architecture is related to ReIG-DQ, but it is not the definition of ReIG-DQ.

Interpretation rule:
Historical ReIG2 claims/code != Frozen ReIG-DQ theorem core != Publication / provenance infrastructure
